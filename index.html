Common questions

What does membership cost?

Jamee Professional Weekly is an auto-renewing in-app subscription. The U.S. App Store price is $2.99 per week; Apple displays the actual price in your region before purchase. There is no free trial. Your Apple ID is billed at confirmation and the subscription renews each week until canceled.

How do I cancel or restore?

To cancel, open your iPhone or iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Jamee, or use Apple's subscription management. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. To restore, use Restore purchases on Jamee's membership screen, then sign into your Jamee account so an eligible Apple purchase can be attached.

Why sign in after buying?

Jamee shows the membership price before login. After Apple's purchase confirmation, sign in or create a Jamee account to link the verified purchase to your workspace. Existing members may sign in first and restore access. Sign in with Apple is available on iOS.

How does Credential Kit connect?

Credential Kit: License Wallet is a separate, optional app. When you choose a one-time handoff, Jamee receives only selected credential summaries. Documents, credential numbers, and private notes stay in Credential Kit. The two apps may require separate accounts and subscriptions.

Can I enter patient information?

No patient information is requested during personal onboarding. Protected clinical drafting and patient handoffs require a separately authorized organization with Agency Secure Mode and appropriate governance. The personal weekly membership alone does not grant that access. Follow your employer's policies; Jamee does not replace your clinical judgment.

Where are privacy and terms?

Review the Jamee privacy policy and Apple's standard end-user license agreement. For account access, data, or billing questions, contact info@jamee.io.

Important: This page describes the Jamee iOS 1.1.3 personal membership. Features available only to an authorized agency are not included in the $2.99 weekly plan. App Store transactions and cancellation are managed by Apple.
