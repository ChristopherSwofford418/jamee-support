# Privacy notice

Effective September 30, 2026

This notice describes how the Jamee iPhone and iPad app handles information for a personal workday account and, separately, for an organization that has been authorized for Agency Secure Mode.

**Personal mode is not a patient record.** Do not enter protected health information, patient names, diagnoses, or clinical documents into personal onboarding, personal tasks, or the personal organizer. Real clinical features require a separately approved agency workspace and appropriate governance.

## Information you provide

When you use Jamee, we may process your email and account identifiers; an optional name, role, specialty, agency name, work setting, region, and goals; and the personal administrative information you enter, such as tasks, credential-renewal summaries, and mileage. Onboarding choices are stored on your device first and may sync to your account after sign-in. The region field is self-reported and is not device GPS tracking in onboarding.

## Sign-in, purchases, and the companion app

Apple may provide an account identifier and, depending on your choices, a name or email when you use Sign in with Apple. Apple processes App Store payments; Jamee receives transaction identifiers and subscription status to verify and associate access with your signed-in account, not your payment-card number. A separately authorized, one-time Credential Kit connection can bring selected credential summary fields to Jamee. It does not import credential documents, numbers, or private notes through that handoff.

## Agency Secure Mode

An organization must separately authorize Agency Secure Mode and establish the required governance, including an applicable Business Associate Agreement, before the app may be used for protected clinical work. In that mode, the agency controls access to its records and applicable retention policies. Do not treat a personal $2.99/week subscription as authorization to process patient data. Clinical drafts and handoffs require human review and must follow the agency's policies.

## Use, service providers, and safeguards

We use information to run account sign-in, save personal preferences and workday data, verify App Store purchases, provide requested connections, and support the service. App data may be processed by infrastructure and authentication providers needed to run Jamee, including Supabase; Apple handles Apple ID sign-in and in-app payments. We use access controls and encrypted network connections. No security system is infallible. Jamee does not automatically transmit personal-mode notes to a general-purpose clinical AI assistant as a feature of the weekly membership.

## Retention and your choices

Personal account and workday data may be kept while your account remains active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal account data through the app's account controls or by contacting [info@jamee.io](mailto:info@jamee.io?subject=Jamee%20privacy%20request). An agency is responsible for its clinical records and applicable healthcare retention duties; contact that organization's privacy officer about its records. Deleting a Jamee account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription—cancel separately in Apple subscriptions.

## Changes and contact

We may update this notice when Jamee changes. The effective date on this page identifies the current notice. For privacy questions, contact [info@jamee.io](mailto:info@jamee.io?subject=Jamee%20privacy%20request). For app help, see [Jamee Support](README.md).
